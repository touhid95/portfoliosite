# About Mahfujul Kader Touhid

This is the landing page of the portfolio — a minimal, precise introduction.

## Character

Curious by nature, disciplined by practice. Touhid believes most things worth knowing sit at the boundary between two fields — and that is exactly where he likes to work.

## Interests

- **Data and its stories** — finding meaning in numbers and patterns
- **Finance as a language** — understanding how capital flows shape decisions
- **Photography as a way of slowing down** — visual storytelling, gallery section titled "Mirror story with lens"
- **Football for the joy of it** — genuine love of the game
- **Debate for the discipline of clarity** — competitive public speaking background

## Practice

BBA student at IBA-JU. Spends most of his time with data — building models, finding patterns, asking better questions. The goal is not a title. It is to understand systems deeply enough to eventually shape them.

## Website Design Philosophy

The portfolio is designed like an architectural firm brochure — minimal, editorial, consistent. Cream background (#FCFAF2), dark text (#2C2C2C), red accent (#BC2026). Typography: Georgia serif + Courier New mono. Gallery section: "Mirror story with lens."
